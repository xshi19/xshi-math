title: Normix theory
---

This track is the prospective home for selected mathematical explanations about
normal mixtures and their uses. Begin with
[conditioning a mixture](./normix-conditioning-a-mixture.md), an original
calculation that fixes the independence and moment assumptions explicitly.

The [normix repository](https://github.com/xshi19/normix) owns the JAX
implementation, public API, package tests, and releases. Its
[package documentation](https://xshi19.github.io/normix/) remains the entry point
for installation and supported operations. This foundation does not install or
vendor that package.

Later computational notes should use a tested upstream version and link the
mathematical convention to its supported API. Selected existing theory pages
still await the separate migration review and legacy URL mapping.
